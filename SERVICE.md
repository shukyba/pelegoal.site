# PeleGoal Service Overview

A service that helps gather contact information from websites for outreach and lead generation.

## How It Works

### 1. Search Phase
- Enter a search phrase relevant to your target audience or niche

### 2. Discovery
- Search using Google or Local Business Search
- Compile a comprehensive list of relevant sites

### 3. Contact Extraction
The service automatically extracts contact information from each site:
- Email addresses
- Social media profiles
- Contact pages

### 4. Autofill Extension
- For contact pages, our browser extension automatically fills in your outreach forms
- Streamlines the process of reaching out to multiple contacts

### 5. AI-Powered Message Customization
- Our AI helps customize your outreach message
- Messages are tailored specifically for each URL/business
- Increases engagement by making outreach feel personal and relevant

## Key Features

- **Automated Search**: Leverage Google and local business directories
- **Contact Extraction**: Emails, socials, and contact pages
- **Browser Extension**: Autofill contact forms
- **AI Personalization**: Tailored messages for each target
